# Coffee Processing — Overview

Processing converts the coffee cherry into exportable green beans. The method chosen fundamentally shapes the flavor potential of the final cup.

---

## Processing Methods Summary

| Method | Depulping | Fermentation | Drying | Mucilage Removed | Flavor Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Washed (Wet) | Yes | Yes (wet) | Raised beds / mechanical | Fully | Clean, bright, terroir-forward |
| Natural (Dry) | No | Yes (aerobic, on fruit) | On fruit | No | Fruity, wine-like, heavy body |
| Honey | Yes | Optional | With mucilage intact | Partially (varies) | Balanced; sweetness varies by honey level |
| White Honey | Yes | Minimal | ~20% mucilage retained | Mostly | Light body, clean, mild sweetness |
| Yellow Honey | Yes | Short | ~50% mucilage | Half | Balanced sweetness, mild fruit |
| Red Honey | Yes | Extended | ~75% mucilage | Less | Richer sweetness, more fruit |
| Black Honey | Yes | Long / controlled | ~100% mucilage | Minimal | Approaching natural; syrupy, fruit-forward |
| Anaerobic (Natural) | No | Yes (anaerobic tank) | On fruit | No | Intense, wine/funk, fermented notes |
| Anaerobic (Washed) | Yes | Yes (anaerobic tank) | Normal | Fully | Clean but complex, floral/tropical |
| Carbonic Maceration | No / Yes | CO₂-sealed, whole cherry | Varies | Varies | Juicy, wine-grape, aromatic |
| Wet-Hulled (Giling Basah) | Yes | Short | Partial before hulling | Fully | Earthy, low acid, heavy body |

> This table is flavor-forward. For a granular **step-by-step decomposition** (10 process steps × 9 methods, with which steps are always/sometimes/rarely present per method), see **Kornman's Processing Flowchart** — now a full searchable table in [[concepts/processing-terminology]].
