# Typica

## Definition

One of the two foundational arabica lineages (alongside [[concepts/bourbon]]). The Typica genetic group comprises all varieties descended from seeds taken from India to Java by the Dutch in 1696–99, which then passed through a single plant in Amsterdam (1706) before colonizing the Americas.

> **Not a mutation of Bourbon.** DNA fingerprinting confirms Typica and Bourbon are parallel lineages from the same Yemen stock. Typica separated from Bourbon when Dutch seeds were moved from India → Java, not when Bourbon mutated. (Source: [[sources/history-of-arabica-wcr]])

## Dispersal Route

Yemen → India (Malabar, ~1670) → Java, Indonesia (1696–99) → Amsterdam botanical garden (1706, a single plant) → Paris / Martinique (1720s) → Americas throughout 18th century.

See [[concepts/arabica-history]] for full timeline.

## Key Characteristics

- Low yield
- Highly susceptible to major coffee diseases (coffee leaf rust, coffee berry disease)
- Standard to high cup quality
- Elongated bean shape; bronze-tipped new growth

## Geographic Distribution

- **Americas:** Dominated Central America until the 1940s; still widely grown in Peru, Dominican Republic, Jamaica
- **Indonesia:** Origin of the lineage; wet-hulled Sumatran coffees often have Typica heritage
- **Latin America (broadly):** Brazil is 97.55% Typica/Bourbon-derived (combined)

## Key Descendants

These varieties are in the Typica genetic group (partially or fully):
- **Bourbon** — parallel lineage from the same Yemen stock (NOT descended from Typica)
- **Caturra** — dwarf Bourbon mutation (Bourbon-side, not directly Typica)
- **Java** — Typica selection
- **Maragogipe** — Typica mutation; giant bean
- **Pache** — Typica natural mutation (compact; Guatemala; best >1,200m) — confirmed WCR 2025
- **SL34** — Kenyan Scott Lab selection; **Typica-related** per WCR DNA fingerprinting (despite shared French Mission origin with SL28 which is Bourbon-related) — exceptional cup quality at altitude
- **Blue Mountain** — Typica selection (Jamaica)
- **Arabigo / Indio / Criollo / Sumatra** — regional alternate names for the Typica group
