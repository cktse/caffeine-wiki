# 德热296 / Dr296 — Yunnan "Purple-Leaf Caturra"

## Definition

A purple-leaf **Catimor** variety from Yunnan, China — bred by **DTARI** (德宏热带农业科学研究所) within the **Catimor CIFC 7963** family and **nationally certified**. Marketed colloquially as **"云南紫叶卡杜拉 / Yunnan Purple-Leaf Caturra,"** which is a **misnomer**: genetically it belongs to the introgressed **[[concepts/introgressed-varieties|Catimor]]** group (Caturra × Timor Hybrid ancestry → *C. canephora* introgression, rust resistance) — **not** a Bourbon-family [[concepts/caturra|Caturra]].

> **Grounding:** the specific claims trace to a single commercial article ([[sources/dr296-guchaju-2024]]), but the **core identity is corroborated** by the official MoA germplasm registry (Dr296 is a real DTARI selection; DTARI's certified stock is Catimor 7963 + the Dr series) and a peer-reviewed RAD-seq study of DTARI's Dehong collection (Bourbon/Typica + Ethiopian + **Introgression/Catimor** groups). So the Catimor classification is solid; the exact parentage and cup notes are Medium/as-claimed.

## Why It Matters

- **Name debunk / sourcing read:** "Purple-Leaf Caturra" implies a delicate Bourbon-Caturra cup; in reality Dr296 is a **rust-resistant Catimor**. Don't assume pure-Caturra genetics or quality. This is why the wiki treats the bag-label "purple leaf caturra" pages as unreliable and prefers the institute-grounded identity.
- **Two different "purple caturras":** Colombia has a *genuine* Purple Caturra (Bourbon lineage); Yunnan's "紫叶卡杜拉" is Dr296 (a Catimor). Same nickname, different genetics — a real sourcing trap.
- **Yunnan specialty angle:** high-grown (1700–1800 m), K72-washed, light-medium roast → the source reports a **clean, tea-like** cup (citrus / red apple / sugarcane / oolong), aroma the weak point.

## Key Facts (as claimed — [[sources/dr296-guchaju-2024]]; identity corroborated)
- Family: **Catimor CIFC 7963**; DTARI-bred; nationally certified. Sibling Dr series: Dr3, Dr132, Dr48-1, Dr199-1.
- Morphology: medium-dwarf; brownish-red new leaves, copper-green mature; large flat dark-red fruit; claimed very large bean (1000-bean ~187 g — unverified).
- Processing: commonly **K72 washed** (Kenyan-style, 72 h, day-dry/night-wet, pH 4–4.1).
- Grown: Dehong, Xishuangbanna (Menghai), Pu'er (Mojiang); ~1700–1800 m.

## Open Questions
- Exact parentage: is Dr296 a direct *mutation* of Catimor 7963, or a selection within the family? Official registry confirms it as a DTARI selection but doesn't state the mutation lineage (that's from guchaju).
- Genetic profile: what *C. canephora* introgression % / does the purple-leaf trait affect anything beyond foliage colour? No genetic study names Dr296 specifically (Frontiers 2025 covers the collection, not the cultivar).
- Is the "largest arabica bean" claim real (vs. Maragogipe/Pacamara)? Single-source.
- Does the K72 protocol here (72 h, day-dry/night-wet, pH 4–4.1) generalize, or is it this producer's version?
