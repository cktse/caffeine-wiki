# Catuaí

## Definition

A Brazilian cultivar bred by IAC from **Mundo Novo × Caturra** (released 1972). It combines Mundo Novo's vigour and yield with **Caturra's dwarf (compact) stature**, enabling high-density planting and mechanized management. Comes in red-fruit (**Catuaí Vermelho**) and yellow-fruit (**Catuaí Amarelo**) forms. High-yielding but input-hungry and disease-susceptible. WCR genetic group: **Bourbon-Typica (Typica *and* Bourbon related)**; WCR quality: "**good**." "Catuaí" is really a group — **13 registered cultivars** carry the name, so producer/BSCA "Catuaí" reports rarely specify which.

## Why It Matters (in this domain)

- **The other defining Brazilian Arabica.** With [[concepts/mundo-novo]], ~80% of Brazil's Arabica; **second most planted** cultivar group on BSCA specialty farms. ([[../sources/brazil-specialty-coffee-sera-2025]])
- **Top-tier capable, unlike its Mundo Novo parent.** Catuaí-group cultivars **dominated the top Cup of Excellence Brazil lots 2019–2022** (ahead of Bourbon Amarelo and Geisha). So among the Brazilian workhorses, Catuaí is the one that reaches competition peaks — relevant for green buying.
- **Cultivar ambiguity caveat:** only **Catuaí Amarelo IAC 62** is specifically documented as quality-differentiated among the 13; a bag labeled "Catuaí" could be any of them. Yellow vs. red is usually the only distinction reported.
