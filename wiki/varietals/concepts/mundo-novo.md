# Mundo Novo

## Definition

A natural hybrid of **Typica (Sumatra) × Bourbon (Bourbon Vermelho)**, discovered in the Mineiros do Tietê region of São Paulo, Brazil, and developed/named by the Instituto Agronômico (IAC) from the 1940s. WCR genetic group: **Bourbon-Typica (Typica *and* Bourbon related)** — it carries both parental lineages. Tall, vigorous, high-yielding, disease-susceptible. **Acaiá** is a set of IAC selections essentially derived from Mundo Novo and is grouped with it.

## Why It Matters (in this domain)

- **One of the two cultivars that define Brazilian Arabica.** Mundo Novo + [[concepts/catuai]] account for ~**80% of Brazil's Arabica** and, with Acaiá, were planted on **110 of 175 BSCA specialty farms** — the most common cultivar group. ([[../sources/brazil-specialty-coffee-sera-2025]])
- **Parent of Catuaí** (Mundo Novo × Caturra) — its vigor + yield crossed onto Caturra's compact dwarf stature.
- **Quality:** WCR rates it "**good**"; Brazilian sensory literature rates it "good" to "fair." Notably, Mundo Novo was **absent from the top Cup of Excellence Brazil lots 2019–2022** — a signal that while it's the workhorse of volume, it is not the peak-quality Brazilian cultivar (Catuaí, Bourbon Amarelo, and introgressed Catucaí outperform it at competition level). This is useful for green buying: "Mundo Novo" on a Brazilian lot signals reliable body/sweetness, not top-of-table complexity.
