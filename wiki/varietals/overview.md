# Coffee Varietals — Overview

Coffee's genetic diversity spans two primary species and hundreds of varietals, each carrying distinct cup characteristics shaped by genetics, altitude, and origin.

---

## Species

| Species | Share of Production | Cup Profile | Caffeine | Key Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arabica (*C. arabica*) | ~60–70% | Complex, aromatic, acidic | Lower (~1.2%) | All specialty coffee is arabica |
| Robusta (*C. canephora*) | ~40% (rose from 25% since early 1990s) | Earthy, bitter, full body; specialty-grade possible with processing | Higher (~2.7%) | Allogamous; cannot self-pollinate; two genetic groups: Guinean and Congolese |
| Liberica / Excelsa | <1% | Woody, smoky, unusual | Moderate | Niche; Philippines, West Africa |

---

## Robusta Genetic Groups (WCR classification)

Two broad genetic groups, confirmed by DNA analysis (Source: [[sources/history-of-robusta-wcr]]):

| Group | Wild Origin | Agronomic Traits | Cup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guinean** | Central-west Africa (Ivory Coast, Guinea, Gabon) | Drought resistant, early harvest, secondary branching, narrow internodes | High caffeine, low bean weight |
| **Congolese** | Central Africa (DRC, Uganda) | Drought susceptible, late harvest, tall, large internodes; **most widespread globally** | Higher rust resistance, high bean weight |

**Key agronomic distinction**: Robusta is **allogamous** (cannot self-pollinate) — plantations must mix compatible clone types. See [[concepts/robusta]].

---

## Arabica Genetic Categories (WCR classification)

Four main categories confirmed by DNA fingerprinting (Source: [[sources/history-of-arabica-wcr]]):

| Category | Origin | Cup quality | Disease resistance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethiopian Landrace | Ethiopia (forests) | Very high; diverse | — |
| Bourbon & Typica Group | Yemen → diverged early 18th C | High | Susceptible |
| Introgressed (Catimor/Sarchimor) | Timor Hybrid × Bourbon descendants | Historically lower; improving | CLR resistant → breaking down |
| F1 Hybrid | Two distinct arabica parents | High; varies | Varies |

## Arabica Varietal Tree (simplified)

> **Correction (2026-06-17):** Previous version said "Bourbon — mutation of Typica." This is incorrect. DNA fingerprinting confirms Bourbon and Typica are **parallel lineages** from Yemen stock. They diverged when Dutch seeds moved from India to Java (1696–99). See [[concepts/typica]] and [[concepts/bourbon]].

**Bourbon lineage** (Yemen → La Réunion → Africa/Americas):
- **Bourbon** — parallel to Typica; sweeter, rounder; isolated on La Réunion until mid-19th C
  - SL28, K7 — Kenyan selections from French Mission coffee; **Bourbon-related** (WCR 2025 DNA)
  - Caturra — Bourbon dwarf mutation (Brazil)
    - Catuaí — Caturra × Mundo Novo hybrid
    - Catimors — Caturra × Timor Hybrid (introgressed; CLR-resistant)
  - Villa Sarchi — Bourbon mutation (Costa Rica)
    - Sarchimors — Villa Sarchi × Timor Hybrid (introgressed)
  - Pacas — Bourbon mutation (El Salvador)
    - Pacamara — Pacas × Maragogipe; large bean, complex

**Typica lineage** (Yemen → India → Java → Amsterdam → Americas):
- **Typica** — colonized the Americas through the 18th C; low yield, disease-susceptible
  - SL34 — Kenyan selection from French Mission coffee; **Typica-related** (WCR 2025 DNA) — despite shared French Mission origin with SL28
  - Maragogipe — Typica mutation; giant bean
  - Pache — Typica natural mutation; compact; large bean; Guatemala; best >1,200m
  - Blue Mountain — Typica selection (Jamaica)

**Ethiopian Landrace** (undispersed):
- **[[concepts/geisha|Gesha / Geisha]]** — Ethiopian origin (Gesha forest, Kaffa/Bench Maji; SW Ethiopia); jasmine, bergamot, tea-like fingerprint; $10,013/kg auction record 2024; diaspora path: Ethiopia → CATIE → Panama
- **[[concepts/chiroso|Chiroso group]]** — three varieties (Caturra Chiroso, Bourbon Chiroso, Chiroso); Eastern Ethiopian origin (Berbere/Bale Mountains area); Urrao, Antioquia, Colombia; 1,950–2,100m; up to $45/pound; names are phenotypic not genetic — NOT mutations of Caturra or Bourbon
- Ethiopian heirlooms (Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Harrar, etc.) — enormous genetic diversity; often unlabeled

**F1 Hybrids** (modern):
- Centroamericano, Milenio — high yield; must propagate clonally (seeds will not breed true)

## Major Growing Regions

- [[entities/ethiopia]] — birthplace of arabica; 6 regions (Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Guji, Harrar, Jimma, Limu); landrace + JARC 74 Series; G1–G5 grading
- [[entities/kenya]] — SL28, SL34; bright, blackcurrant acidity
- [[entities/colombia]] — Caturra, Castillo (87% of crops), Variedad Colombia, Tabi, Pink Bourbon, **Chiroso group** (Ethiopian Only; Urrao; 1,950–2,100m); dual harvest; 22 regions; 1,200–2,300m
- [[entities/guatemala]] — Bourbon + Caturra dominant; 8 regions; all SHB (>1,300m); primarily washed; Antigua, Huehuetenango most notable
- [[entities/panama]] — Gesha heartland
- [[entities/costa-rica]] — 8 regions (Tarrazú flagship); honey processing association; SHB/GHB/MHB grading; ~30-year Robusta ban (lifted 2018); ICAFE governed
- [[entities/yemen]] — oldest cultivated arabica; Mocha port heritage
- [[entities/indonesia]] — 4th largest producer; Sumatra dominant; Wet Hulling (Giling Basah); Catimor/Ateng, Typica, S795; Aceh/Gayo (EU PGI), Lintong; medium-dark roast
- [[entities/brazil]] — **largest producer** (~⅓ of world; 66% Arabica); Mundo Novo + Catuaí = ~80% of Arabica; introgressed (HdT + Icatu) cultivars reach 90+ at COE; semidry/pulped-natural (CD) dominant; Minas Gerais = 82% of specialty area; Cerrado/Sul de Minas/Mogiana/Mantiqueira
