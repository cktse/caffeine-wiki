# 德热296 / Dr296 — "Yunnan Purple-Leaf Caturra" (Guchaju, 2024)

## Summary

Chinese coffee content article (guchaju.com, Dec 2024) on **德热296 (Dr296)**, colloquially "云南紫叶卡杜拉 / Yunnan Purple-Leaf Caturra." Its central claim corrects a common misnomer: **Dr296 is not a Bourbon-Caturra** — it's a purple-leaf variety in the **Catimor CIFC 7963** family, bred by **DTARI** (德宏热带农业科学研究所 / Dehong Tropical Agriculture Research Institute), i.e. an **introgressed (Catimor) variety** — Robusta-containing, rust-resistant — despite the "Caturra" name. Also documents its K72-washed protocol, morphology, and cup profile.

## Credibility — Medium-High (corroborated)

The guchaju article on its own is Low-Medium (commercial content-and-retail site, single article). But its **core identity claim is independently corroborated**:

- **Official MoA germplasm registry** — [ctcgris.cn](https://ctcgris.cn/content-153.html) (中国热带作物种质资源信息网, 农业农村部瑞丽咖啡种质资源圃) confirms **Dr296 is a real DTARI-bred selection** ("初步选育出德热296…有望推广的新品种") and that DTARI's *certified* varieties include **Catimor CIFC 7963 + the Dr series** (Dr3, Dr132); Catimor is listed as an *introgression type*.
- **Peer-reviewed** — Frontiers in Plant Science 2025 (RAD-seq) studied **DTARI's own Dehong 185-accession collection**, sorting it into Bourbon/Typica, Ethiopian, and **Introgression (Catimor/Sarchimor)** groups; confirms Catimor = Caturra × Timor.
- **English trade context** — Yunnan cultivation is overwhelmingly Catimor; a **Dehong "Catimor P4 (7963)" at ~1,700 m** matches Dr296's stated parent + altitude.

**Net:** the classification that matters — *Dr296 is a real DTARI Yunnan Catimor (introgressed), not a Bourbon-Caturra* — is **well-grounded**. Only the exact "mutation of Catimor 7963" parentage rests mainly on guchaju + strong institutional consistency (Medium). Cup/flavour and the "largest bean" claim remain single-source **as-claimed**.

## Key Claims (as claimed)

### Genetic identity — the correction
- Dr296 = a **purple-leaf variety in the Catimor 7963 (CIFC 7963)** family, bred by **DTARI**; **nationally certified** → genetically an **introgressed Catimor**, *not* a pure Caturra.
- Sibling Dr series: **Dr3 (德热3号)** (found 1995, Lujiangba; certified 2018); registry also lists **Dr132, Dr48-1, Dr199-1**.
- vs. Colombia's genuine **Purple Caturra** (a real Bourbon-lineage Caturra colour variant): Dr296 merely *resembles* it (more copper/purple foliage) — the shared name is visual/marketing, not genetic.
- ⇒ "Yunnan Purple-Leaf Caturra" is a **misnomer**: Caturra sits in Catimor's ancestry, but Dr296 is Robusta-introgressed and rust-oriented.

### Morphology / agronomy
- Medium-dwarf shrub; new leaves brownish-red, mature copper-green; large flat fruit, dark-red ripe; ~3 yrs to first seed; yr-1 yield ~1.91 kg fresh/plant.
- **1000-bean weight 187 g @ 10.7% moisture — claimed "largest bean among known arabica varieties."** ⚠ single-source, unverified (cf. Maragogipe/Pacamara).
- Grown in Dehong (as landscape trees), Xishuangbanna (Menghai), Pu'er (Mojiang); **~1700–1800 m**. German (Berlin) cupping eval: *medium* quality on an 800 m sample.

### Processing — K72 washed (as described here)
"Kenyan-style" refined washed — longer soak, longer fermentation, repeated washing:
1. Ripe cherries → spring-water flotation.
2. Depulp, **keep mucilage**, **24 h dry ferment (no water)**.
3. Next day: wash mucilage; U-channel repeated washing; **second ferment** — **day dry / night wet**; monitor humidity; ferment to **pH 4–4.1**; **72 h total**.
4. Wash; dry on **shaded raised beds, low-temp slow drying**.

### Roast & cup (as claimed, single-source)
- Roast **light-medium**. Brew rec: **91 °C ±1 · medium grind · ~1:50 ±10 s · 1:15**.
- Clean, layered; acidity lemon/kumquat/citrus; sweetness papaya/apple + **sugarcane** aftertaste; **oolong (Tieguanyin) / black-tea** body; brighter cooled; **aroma slightly weak**.
