# Ethiopia Sidama Coffee — 1Zpresso (2026)

## Summary

A 2026 brand marketing article from 1Zpresso focused on the Sidama region: terroir, varieties (heirloom, Geisha, 74 Series), processing, flavor profile, grading, and a Sidama vs. Yirgacheffe comparison. **Source credibility: Low-Medium** — same 1Zpresso publisher as the Colombian and Ethiopian coffee guides. No Bourbon/Typica error this time. The 74 Series origin story (CBD crisis 1971 → research response → released 1974) is substantive and not in the prior source. Sidama production share (13%) and GI status are new data points.

## Key Claims

- Sidama: 1,550–2,200m; volcanic soil; abundant rainfall; large diurnal temperature variation
- Sidama accounts for ~13% of Ethiopia's total coffee production (USDA Coffee Annual 2024/25)
- 9 of top 10 finishers at the 2024 Cup of Excellence came from Sidama
- Sidama holds geographic indication (GI) or trademark-style protection alongside Yirgacheffe and Harrar — a distinction only a handful of Ethiopian regions have earned
- ~60% of Sidama's coffee is washed; natural is meaningful but secondary; honey and anaerobic exist but are "considerably less common"
- Sidama cup: bright citrus-driven acidity, delicate sweetness; jasmine, bergamot, stone fruit aromatics; smooth, fuller body. Washed lots = clarity + floral brightness; natural = deeper fruit sweetness + heavier mouthfeel
- Sidama suits light roast to preserve brightness and florals; dark roast suppresses these in favor of cocoa/caramel/smoke
- **74 Series origin**: In 1971, Coffee Berry Disease (CBD) severely impacted Ethiopia's coffee industry. In response, research institutions launched a systematic collection and evaluation program for indigenous varieties. 13 selections resulted; the "74 Series" was released in **1974** — the year the series was released gives it its name. Varieties include 74110, 74158, and **74112**
- Geisha: 2004 Hacienda La Esmeralda won Best of Panama competition — this is what put Geisha on the global map (confirms and grounds the origin story)
- Brewing: pour-over is the recommended brewing method for Sidama's brightness and layered aromatics

## Sidama vs. Yirgacheffe (source comparison table)

| | Sidama | Yirgacheffe |
|---|---|---|
| Altitude | 1,550–2,200m | 1,900–2,200m |
| Processing | Primarily washed, some natural | Washed and natural |
| Cup | Balanced acidity + sweetness; jasmine/fruit aromatics; fuller body | Intense floral + fruity; citrus acidity; tea-like lightness |
| Best for | Full, fruit-forward complexity with body | Intense florals and lighter aromatic cup |

Yirgacheffe was formerly part of the Sidama zone; its 1,900m floor makes it higher on average.
