# Coffee Varieties Produced and Marketed in Kenya

## Summary

A 2016 Kenya Coffee Traders Association reference document covering Kenya's five main commercial varieties: SL28, SL34, K7, Ruiru 11, and Batian. Provides basic agronomic descriptions — altitude range, shoot tip color, disease susceptibility, brief cup quality notes. **Source credibility: Low-Medium.** Published by a trade body (not a research institution); predates WCR's 2025 DNA fingerprinting that corrected SL34's genetic lineage. Useful for variety cultivation basics and as the primary basis for creating the Kenya entity page.

## Key Claims

- SL28, SL34, and K7 all trace to French Mission coffee; Blue Mountain also long-established in Kenya
- Source describes French Mission as "a bourbon derivative" → **superseded** by WCR 2025 DNA fingerprinting: SL34 is Typica-related; K7 is Bourbon-related. Do not propagate the blanket Bourbon claim for SL34
- **SL28**: selected from Tanganyika Drought Resistant; medium to high altitude; green shoot tips (occasionally bronze); bold beans; fine liquor; susceptible to CBD, CLR, BBC
- **SL34**: selected from French Mission; dark bronze shoot tips; high altitude, high rainfall adapted; high yields; susceptible to CBD, CLR, BBC
- **K7** (new to wiki): selected at Lengetet Estate in Muhoroni from French Mission; partial CLR resistance to some races; partial CBD resistance; suited for low altitude zones where CLR is prevalent; good bean and cup quality; spreading habit of young laterals; intermediate bronze shoot tips
- **Ruiru 11**: released 1985; CBD and CLR resistant; compact growth (2,500–3,300 trees/ha vs. 1,300 for SL varieties); "quality attributes similar to traditional varieties"
- **Batian**: released 2010; described as "true breeding arabica bred through intensive backcrossing"; tall, similar to SL28 in appearance; large bold beans; high yield; good cup quality

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- **SL34 lineage**: Source claims "French Mission is a bourbon derivative" → WCR 2025 DNA fingerprinting confirms SL34 is **Typica-related** despite French Mission origin. Our wiki follows WCR. Source error is a pre-DNA-era gap, not a deliberate inaccuracy.
- **Ruiru 11 propagation**: Source describes as a "one way cross"; no mention of clonal propagation. WCR 2025 classifies Ruiru 11 as "F1 hybrid (introgressed)" requiring clonal propagation. Treat WCR as authoritative on propagation requirements.
- **Batian "true breeding"**: Source says Batian is "true breeding arabica" bred through backcrossing. WCR classifies Batian under "Introgressed (Other)" — not as an F1 hybrid. No direct contradiction: backcross breeding does produce stable, seed-reproducible varieties, consistent with WCR's non-F1 classification.
- **K7 CLR resistance mechanism**: Source says "resistant to some races of CLR" but K7 is Bourbon-derived (no Timor Hybrid introgression). This is natural partial resistance within the Bourbon-Typica gene pool — distinct from and predating the Catimor introgressed resistance approach.
