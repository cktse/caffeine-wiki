# WCR Arabica Varieties Catalog (2025)

## Summary

Open-access reference catalog of ~70 Arabica (and one Robusta grafting rootstock) coffee varieties from 18 countries. Developed in consultation with national coffee research institutions across Central America and Africa, based on visits to 16 countries and interviews with ~180 experts. Updated September 2025. This is the authoritative WCR companion to the History of Arabica source already in this wiki.

**Source credibility**: Highest available. WCR's DNA fingerprinting and multi-country field trials (23 sites, 15 countries) are the gold standard for varietal performance data. The catalog is a living document — periodically updated.

**How to use**: This catalog is a reference database. For any variety bag you encounter, look it up here to find: genetic group, quality potential at altitude, CLR resistance status, and whether it needs clonal propagation. See [[concepts/arabica-variety-taxonomy]] for the variable schema and key variety quick-reference table.

## Structural Findings (applicable across the wiki)

### 1. SL34 ≠ Bourbon related
The catalog confirms SL28 and SL34 have different genetic backgrounds despite both being "Kenyan selections":
- **SL28**: Bourbon-Typica group (Bourbon related) — selection of "Tanganika Drought Resistant"
- **SL34**: Bourbon-Typica group (**Typica related**) — Typica-like genetic background

Our wiki's `bourbon.md` previously grouped both under French Mission Bourbon. SL34's Typica lineage is corrected in that page.

### 2. Pache = Natural mutation of Typica
Pache (found in 3 Guatemalan regions in the Hoffmann source) is confirmed as a Typica natural mutation: compact stature, large bean, good quality at high altitude, optimal altitude = High, CLR susceptible. Best above 1,200m.

### 3. CLR resistance breakdown — three Catimor varieties confirmed susceptible
All three share the same lineage (Timor Hybrid 832/1 × Caturra):
- **Lempira** (Honduras) — confirmed susceptible in Honduras
- **Costa Rica 95** (Costa Rica) — confirmed susceptible in Costa Rica and possibly elsewhere
- **IHCAFE 90** (Honduras) — confirmed susceptible in Honduras

### 4. Pink Bourbon absent from catalog
Pink Bourbon does not appear anywhere in the 2025 catalog. WCR states that to qualify as a variety it must be "distinct, uniform, and stable." Pink Bourbon's absence confirms the 1Zpresso source's note that "its genetic origin remains uncertain" — it doesn't meet the standard for catalog inclusion.

### 5. F1 hybrid propagation constraint
Every F1 hybrid entry includes the same explicit warning: seeds from hybrid plants will not breed true (segregation). Varieties requiring clonal propagation: Casiopea, Centroamericano, Esperanza, Evaluna, H3, Milenio, Mundo Maya, Nayarita, Ruiru 11, Starmaya.

### 6. Variant Timor Hybrid accessions
Two distinct Timor Hybrid accessions appear in lineages:
- **Timor Hybrid 832/1** → used in all Catimor crosses (Caturra parent)
- **Timor Hybrid 832/2** → used in all Sarchimor crosses (Villa Sarchi parent)
- **Timor Hybrid 1343** → used in Catimor 129

### 7. Typica aliases documented
WCR lists Typica's regional aliases: Criollo (Creole), Indio (Indian), Arábigo (Arabica), Plume Hidalgo, Blue Mountain, Sumatra. Useful for identifying Typica on bags with local names.
