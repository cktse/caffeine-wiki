# World Coffee Research (WCR)

## What / Who

A 501(c)(5) non-profit collaborative research and development program of the global coffee industry. Mission: grow, protect, and enhance supplies of quality coffee while improving livelihoods of producing families.

## Relevance

The authoritative institution for arabica varietal genetics, disease resistance tracking, and variety catalogs. The WCR Variety Catalog (varieties.worldcoffeeresearch.org) is the primary reference for genetically validated varietal information.

## Key Facts

- Maintains the WCR Arabica Variety Catalog — genetically verified varietal data
- Uses DNA fingerprinting to validate varietal identity and historical dispersal claims
- Tracks coffee leaf rust resistance breakdown globally; updates variety status only on validated scientific confirmation
- Works with TaCRI (Tanzanian Coffee Research Institute) and national research bodies
