# FNC — Federación Nacional de Cafeteros de Colombia

## What / Who

The National Federation of Coffee Growers of Colombia. Founded 1927 by local farmers as a nonprofit cooperative. The central organizing and regulatory body for Colombian coffee, with a mandate to ensure long-term prosperity of coffee producers while promoting economic and social well-being in coffee-growing communities.

## Relevance

The FNC sets Colombia's export grading standards (Excelso), manages the "Café de Colombia" certification mark, conducts agricultural research, supports Cenicafé (the national coffee research center), and manages the global marketing of Colombian coffee. It is the institutional reason Colombian coffee maintains consistent quality and brand recognition globally.

## Key Facts

- Founded: 1927
- Type: Non-profit cooperative
- Oversees the **Excelso** export grading standard (bean size, moisture, defects)
- Owns the **Café de Colombia** mark — guarantees 100% Colombian origin
- Funds and coordinates with **Cenicafé** — which developed the Castillo variety and manages CLR resistance breeding
- Manages agricultural research, technical assistance, and purchasing/marketing systems for farmers
- Tracks ~880,000 hectares of cultivation across 22 regions (Source: [[sources/colombian-coffee-guide-1zpresso]])
