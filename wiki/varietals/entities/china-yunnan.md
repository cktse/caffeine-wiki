# Yunnan, China

## What / Who

Yunnan is the southwestern Chinese province that grows the large majority (~95%+) of China's coffee. Key producing prefectures are **Pu'er** (formerly Simao), **Baoshan**, and **Dehong**, near the borders with Myanmar and Laos. Typical altitudes run ~1,000–2,000 m; the specialty tier sits at the upper end (this wiki's first Yunnan lot is 1,720–1,800 m).

## Relevance

- **Emerging specialty origin.** Historically Yunnan was **Catimor-dominated** commodity coffee (rust-resistant, high-yield, sold into instant/blends). A specialty movement over the 2010s–2020s has introduced quality-focused varieties (Caturra, Bourbon, Typica, SL34, and some Geisha) and **experimental processing** (extended/controlled ferments such as K72, plus anaerobic and honey work).
- **Actionable read:** high-grown Yunnan **washed** lots behave like other high-altitude washed coffees — bright, floral, clean — and reward a clarity-forward brew with full extraction for sweetness. See worked example [[../brewing/sources/yunnan-purple-caturra-k72-label]].
