# JARC — Jimma Agricultural Research Center

## What / Who

Ethiopian government agricultural research institution based in Jimma (SW Ethiopia). Responsible for developing improved coffee varieties from Ethiopian genetic stock for domestic cultivation.

## Relevance

JARC developed the **74 Series** — the most commercially significant group of formally bred Ethiopian arabica varieties. These are distinct from unselected Ethiopian landrace: intentionally bred for high yield, disease resistance, and cup quality, using Ethiopian genetic material. JARC varieties compete alongside landrace coffees in the specialty market.

## Key Facts

- **74 Series origin**: In 1971, Coffee Berry Disease (CBD) severely impacted Ethiopia's coffee industry. Research institutions launched a systematic collection and evaluation program of indigenous Ethiopian varieties, screening for disease resistance and cup quality. Of 13 selections that resulted, the "74 Series" was released in **1974** — the year of release is the source of the name. Source: [[sources/ethiopia-sidama-1zpresso]]
- **74 Series varieties** (most notable):
  - **74110**: Floral clarity, fruit-forward sweetness; strong specialty competition results
  - **74158**: Balanced profile; recognized for natural balance and sweetness
  - **74112**: Third notable variety from the same series; Sidama competition track record
  - Bred for: high yield + disease resistance + cup quality — all three, unlike most resistance-focused breeding which sacrifices cup quality
- JARC varieties are *improved varieties*, not landraces — selected and stable, unlike the uncharacterized Ethiopian heirloom population
- 74 Series coffees from Sidama compete successfully in international specialty competitions
- Jimma region (where JARC is based) is itself a major coffee-producing zone: 1,400–2,100m; natural process dominant; tropical fruit and wine-like cup profile
