## Cross-Domain Connections

Origin + varietal + processing → terroir precursors → roasting (expression layer) → brewing (final extraction). See `wiki/shared/` for pages that bridge multiple domains.

The central chain: **green bean selection** (varietal, origin, processing) drives what compounds are latent in the bean; **roasting** amplifies or suppresses those precursors; **brewing** determines how they reach the cup.
