# 2025 "New Hybrid" Hario Switch Recipe by Tetsu Kasuya

## Summary

Kasuya's 2025 update to his "devil" Switch recipe, described as producing "a very sweet cup." The structural innovation is starting with the switch **closed** — creating an immersion bloom rather than a percolation bloom — before switching to open pour-over, then closing again for a final cool-water immersion phase. This three-phase open/close/open/close sequence is more complex than the original devil recipe (two-phase) and produces a noticeably sweeter result.

## Key Claims

- Explicitly described as an updated version of the devil recipe
- Flavor target: "very sweet cup"
- The immersion bloom (switch closed at start) is the key structural departure from the devil recipe
- Temperature drop (90°C → 70-80°C) retained from the devil recipe but applied later and to a narrower temperature window

## The Recipe

**Parameters:**
| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Dose | 20 g |
| Water | 300 ml |
| Ratio | 1:15 |
| Temp (phases 1–2) | 90°C |
| Temp (phase 3) | 70–80°C |
| Grind | Medium-fine |
| Total time | 3:30 |

**Preparation:** Insert and rinse filter. Add ground coffee. Zero scales. Prepare cold drinking water. **Close the switch.**

**Pour sequence:**

| Time | Action | Cumulative water | Switch | Temp |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Add 40–50 g | 40–50 g | **Closed** | 90°C |
| 0:10 | Wait | — | Closed | — |
| 0:35 | Open the switch (drain) | — | **Open** | — |
| 0:40 | Add to 120 g | 120 g | Open | 90°C |
| 0:50 | Wait | — | Open | — |
| 1:30 | Add to 200 g | 200 g | Open | 90°C |
| 1:40 | Cool kettle to 70–80°C | — | Open | cooling |
| 2:05 | Close the switch | — | **Closed** | — |
| 2:10 | Add to 300 g (100 g at 70–80°C) | 300 g | Closed | 70–80°C |
| 2:20 | Wait | — | Closed | — |
| 2:45 | Open switch; drawdown | — | **Open** | — |
| 3:30 | Done | | | |

**Phase summary:**
1. **Immersion bloom** (0:00–0:35, switch closed, 40–50g @ 90°C) — sweet compounds extracted into standing water before any bypass
2. **Percolation** (0:35–2:05, switch open, to 200g @ 90°C) — standard pour-over extraction phase
3. **Cool immersion** (2:10–2:45, switch closed, 100g @ 70–80°C) — tempered final extraction, avoids bitterness

## Why the Closed Bloom Matters

The devil recipe blooms with the switch open — hot water saturates grounds and immediately drains, providing agitation. The 2025 recipe blooms closed — water pools and steeps, extracting sweet soluble compounds (sugars, certain acids) into standing water that is then drained at 0:35 before the main percolation pours. This front-loads sweetness and delays astringent/bitter extraction until the third phase when temperature is already lowered. See [[concepts/neo-switch]] for comparison with the devil recipe.
