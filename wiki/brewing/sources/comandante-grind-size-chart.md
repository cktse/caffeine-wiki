# Comandante Grind Size Chart — Coffee Grind Sizes For Brew Methods

## Summary

Reference guide for grind sizes on the Comandante C40 and C60, with micron values and Red Clix conversion. Covers the full range from Turkish/espresso through pour-over to cold brew. Also includes a practical dialing-in variable priority order and grind texture descriptions. From the same author as the Graycano recipes — click counts in that source map directly to this chart.

## Key Claims

- "No such thing as one grind size for each brew method" — density, roast level, and personal preference all affect the correct grind
- All pour-over methods (V60, Graycano, Kalita Wave, Orea) fall in the same C40 range: 20–30 clicks
- Red Clix doubles the click resolution on the C40 (multiply C40 clicks × 2 for Red Clix equivalent)
- C60: 21µm per click (GX50 Gold Clix system); more granular than C40 (~30µm/click)
- For espresso on C40, Red Clix is strongly recommended for the finer adjustment control needed

| Brew Method | C40 Clicks | Red Clix | Microns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turkish (Ibrik) | 0–3 | 0–6 | 0–90 |
| Espresso | 3–6 | 6–12 | 90–180 |
| AeroPress (espresso-style) | 10–20 | 20–40 | 300–600 |
| Orea V3 | 20–30 | 40–60 | 600–900 |
| Kalita Wave | 20–30 | 40–60 | 600–900 |
| Graycano Dripper | 20–30 | 40–60 | 600–900 |
| Hario V60 | 20–30 | 40–60 | 600–900 |
| AeroPress (filter-style) | 20–30 | 40–60 | 600–900 |
| Cupping | 25–30 | 50–60 | 750–900 |
| Cold Brew (drip) | 25–30 | 50–60 | 750–900 |
| Cold Brew (immersion) | 30–40 | 60–80 | 900–1,200 |

## C40 to C60 Conversion

| Brew Method | C40 | C60 | Microns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turkish (Ibrik) | 0–3 | 0–4 | 0–90 |
| Espresso | 3–6 | 4–9 | 90–180 |
| Pour-over (all methods) | 20–30 | 28–42 | 600–900 |

## Grind Texture Reference

- **Medium-fine** (20–40 C40 clicks): texture like "between table salt and fine sand" — slight coarseness, not powder

## Validation Against Previous Sources

- Graycano cone recipe: 25 clicks → 750µm (within 600–900µm pour-over range ✓)
- Graycano wave recipe: 30 clicks → 900µm (upper end of 600–900µm range ✓)
- Both within this chart's 20–30 click Graycano recommendation — consistent

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- No contradictions with existing wiki.
- The chart groups all pour-over methods (V60, Graycano, Kalita, Orea) at the same 20–30 click range despite their very different flow rates. The Graycano recipe source gave 25 vs. 30 clicks for cone vs. wave modes — this internal variation within the 20–30 range is the practical nuance.
- The C40 µm per click can be back-calculated: 20 clicks = 600µm → 30µm/click. Red Clix halves this to 15µm/click. This is not stated explicitly but is derivable.
- V60 section says "medium-speed flowing dripper" — somewhat at odds with the common characterization of V60 as fast and unforgiving. The 20–30 click range is consistent though.
