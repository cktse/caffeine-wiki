# The BEST Pourover Filter Coffee Recipes 2026 (Harmony compilation)

## Summary

A UK roaster (Harmony Coffee / Ben Rowe) compiles the **"brand-new coffee, one attempt, what's your go-to?"** filter recipe from ~11 competition-level pros (Brewers Cup / Cup Tasters / Aeropress finalists, Q-graders, SCA judges), plus a synthesis of common patterns. Functionally a **practitioner field-test of the wiki's brewing model** ([[brewing/overview]]): the independent recipes cluster around the same reasoning the model encodes. Credibility: **practitioner-survey (medium-high)** with **commercial bias** (roaster blog, product/affiliate links); individual recipes are first-party, "as-stated."

## Consensus starting recipe (their "if new, start here")
15 g / 250 g / **93 °C** / medium grind / 30–45 s bloom / 2:30–3:00 total; **adjust grind first**. Range across pros: brewer V60/Orea/Kalita/Clever; ratio **1:10–1:18**; temp **91–96 °C**; bloom 2×–3× dose.

## Key Claims (patterns)

- **Grind/PSD > temperature.** The strongest recurring theme. Junchao Huang: temperature is *not* the key factor — **particle size distribution** is (targets 600–800 µm); most pros pick a sensible 91–96 °C and adjust grind. → corroborates [[brewing/concepts/particle-size-distribution]] + temp-secondary (Batali, [[brewing/overview]] §7).
- **Roast decides the method** (Gage Quinn, cleanest statement): *"lighter roast is less soluble → prefers percolation, hotter water, wider ratios (more energy/water/agitation)."* → corroborates roast-first framing ([[brewing/overview]] §5; espresso roast→ratio in [[brewing/concepts/espresso-fundamentals]]).
- **Target = balance / "juicy."** Natdanai Denham: *"light-medium body, well extracted, juicy… balances clarity and sweetness while avoiding over-extraction."* Gage: 1.30 % TDS = *"optimal balance between intensity and flavour clarity."* → the model's **X-target** (aim for the juicy centre).
- **No single "correct" recipe; pros split by priority** (sweetness / clarity / body / flavour separation / consistency). → the [[brewing/concepts/brewing-control-chart|BCC]] two-cluster premise and the model's **Y = a choice**.
- **Ratio = strength slider, incl. bypass.** Spread 1:10–1:18; Sharon Ip runs 1:13.5 for "punchiness" and does **post-brew bypass "if too cramped."** → [[brewing/concepts/bypass]], **Z** slider ([[brewing/overview]] §3).
- **Dose ↔ bed depth.** Ben: small doses (12–13 g) taste *"dialled down to 7/10 intensity"*; others love 12–13 g *for clarity/less waste*. Same lever, opposite Y-goals — resolved by [[brewing/concepts/bed-depth]] (shallow bed → less body, finicky).
- **Immersion is forgiving.** Ted Longden's Clever Dripper (all water at once, no bloom, "8/10 100 % of the time") — extraction mostly immersion, CO₂ off-gassed before percolation. → [[brewing/entities/clever-dripper]], [[brewing/concepts/neo-switch]].
- **Flat-bottom brewers gaining ground.** Kalita / Orea / FLO recur as *balanced, forgiving, even, lower-channeling*. → new [[brewing/concepts/flat-bottom-drippers]] (the one real gap this source surfaces).
- **Lower temps trending back** (91–94 °C): *"trading aromatic intensity for flavour clarity."*
- **Bloom ratio as a lever** (Natdanai): 3× bloom for dense/high-CO₂ light roasts, but can trade sweetness for clarity + stall; 2× keeps it balanced/approachable.
- **Origin density → grind** (FAQ): high-density origins (Colombia, Peru, Ethiopia, Kenya) → grind 1–2 steps coarser. → altitude/density modifier ([[brewing/overview]] §2).

## Representative recipes (as-stated)
- **Cleo Tsai** (Canadian Brewers Cup finalist) — V60, 15 g/250 g, 93 °C, 4× even pours (60/60/60/70), 2:40; flat-bottom Orea for anaerobic/"crazy" processed.
- **Gage Quinn** — Orea V3, 12 g/200 g, 94 °C, 25 clk; targets ~1.30 % TDS.
- **Ted Longden** — Clever, 15 g/250 g, 94 °C, all-in + stir, drain 2:30.
- **Ben (Harmony)** — Orea V4 + Sibarist fast-flow, 16 g/250 g, 94 °C, 26 clk, ~2:00 (fast, clarity-led).

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- Temps cluster 91–96 °C — not a conflict with Kasuya's 93/88/83 (these pros brew light-medium specialty, not dark).
- Water composition varies widely (50–120 ppm; **APAX** drops recur — [[brewing/entities/apax-lab]]) — **out of scope here** (HK water treated as near-neutral).
- Recurring but hearsay-level: flat-bottom = higher extraction — see the bias note on [[brewing/concepts/flat-bottom-drippers]].
