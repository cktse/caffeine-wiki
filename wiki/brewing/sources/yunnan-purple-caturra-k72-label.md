# Yunnan Purple Leaf Caturra, K72 Washed — Bag Label + Dialed Recipe

## Summary

A worked brewing example: a Yunnan (China) washed coffee dialed in on the V60 using a sweet 4:6 profile plus a dilution bypass. Records the roaster's own brew recommendation (with critique), the recipe CK actually landed on, and the measured extraction. This is the wiki's first entry for [[varietals/entities/china-yunnan]], [[varietals/concepts/purple-leaf-caturra]], and [[processing/concepts/k72-washed]], and the anchoring example for [[brewing/concepts/bypass]].

## The Coffee (label)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Flavor notes | White Floral, Melon, Green Tea, Spice |
| Variety | Purple Leaf Caturra |
| Process | K72 Washed |
| Origin | Yunnan, China |
| Altitude | 1,720–1,800 m |
| Roast level | 2 / 5 (light-medium) |

Profile reads as a **delicate, tea-like, high-grown washed** coffee → maps to the Ethiopia/Kenya Washed row of [[brewing/overview]] (highlight acidity + florals; V60/Origami; 1:15–1:16; 91–93°C; 4:6 bright/sweet).

## Roaster's Brew Recommendation — and critique

| Param | Roaster | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Ratio | **1:13** | ⚠️ Strong. With their 1.15–1.35% TDS target this implies only **~13–15% EY** — deliberately low extraction; sacrifices sweetness/body. Prefer **1:15**. |
| Water temp | 89–92°C | Slightly low for a light-*medium* dense washed bean; sit at **92–93°C** to develop sweetness + florals. |
| Brew time | 1–1.5 min | ⚠️ Very fast for filter; drives the low EY. Legitimate clarity/anti-astringency play but under-extracts if sweetness is the goal. |
| TDS | 1.15–1.35% | Reasonable strength; it's the *pairing with 1:13* that signals low EY, not the TDS itself. |
| Water | 50–110 PPM (Nongfu ~60) | 110 too high for florals; keep **~40–70 PPM**. Nongfu ~60 is a good pick. |

**Bottom line:** the roaster's recipe is a safe, bright, clarity-first profile that under-delivers on sweetness — buying clarity by *under-extracting*. CK's version buys clarity by *diluting a fully-extracted brew* instead (see below).

## Dialed Recipe (CK, validated)

Option A sweet 4:6 ([[brewing/concepts/46-method]]) + dilution bypass ([[brewing/concepts/bypass]]).

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Dripper | Hario V60 ceramic |
| Dose | 15 g |
| Brew ratio (through bed) | **1:15 → 225 g** |
| Bypass | **+25 g to the cup → 250 g total water** |
| Grinder | **Timemore O78s @ 17** (calibrated: zero = just above burr touch) |
| Temperature | 92–93°C |
| Water | soft, ~60 PPM (Nongfu-type) |

**Pour schedule** (40% flavor phase, sweet split first < second; 60% strength in 3 pours for medium body — Option A scaled to 15 g / 225 g):

| Time | Pour | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 40 g | 40 g |
| 0:45 | 50 g | 90 g (40% phase, sweet) |
| 1:30 | 45 g | 135 g |
| 2:15 | 45 g | 180 g |
| 2:45 | 45 g | 225 g (60% phase, medium body) |
| after drawdown | +25 g bypass to cup | 250 g total |

## Measured Result

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Final beverage | ~215 g (⇒ ~35 g retained, ~2.3 g/g) |
| TDS | **1.43%** |
| **EY** | **~20.5%** (215 × 1.43% / 15) |
| Sensory | Good balance of sweetness and clarity; "still more coffee-like than tea-like" |

EY ~20.5% sits at the upper-middle of the classic 18–22% band — vs. the roaster's implied ~14%. The bypass (~10% of the cup) dropped strength ~1.62% → 1.43% while leaving that extraction intact. **Sweetness from real extraction; clarity from dilution.**

## Key Claims
- At 1:13 + short contact, the roaster's own TDS/ratio numbers imply low EY (~14%) — under-extraction, not a sweetness-forward recipe.
- Brewing at 1:15 (EY ~20.5%) then bypassing ~10% delivers the roaster's intended clarity *without* sacrificing sweetness — a better mechanism for the same goal.
- Bypass % is a strength dial independent of the 4:6 flavor/strength axes.
