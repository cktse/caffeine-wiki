# Hario Switch Review: The Hybrid Brewer You Need to Try

## Summary

Review of the original Hario Switch (glass V60 cone on a silicone switch base) by Asser Christensen. The core argument is that the Switch is not a Clever Dripper clone — it's a genuine hybrid that combines pour-over percolation agitation with immersion steeping. The article presents a specific 50/50 pour-split recipe that Christensen developed and that has become widely adopted.

## Key Claims

- The Switch uses a steel ball + silicone base + plastic lever: the lever pokes the ball to block/unblock drain flow. Feels slightly flimsy but works reliably in practice.
- Compatible with 02-size V60 filters. A 03-size Switch exists but is rare.
- Other cones can be seated on the Switch base (Hario Mugen 03 works; steel V60 leaked).
- Distinction from Clever Dripper: Clever uses trapezoid filters and is primarily immersion-only; Switch uses V60 cone filters and enables true hybrid technique.
- Tetsu Kasuya popularized the "devil Switch technique" — Christensen's recipe is a modification tuned for lighter/Nordic roasts with higher extraction than Kasuya's original.

## The Coffee Chronicler Recipe

**Core structure (scales from 15–26g):**

| Step | Time | Action |
|---|---|---|
| First pour | 0:00 | 50% of total water — Switch OPEN (percolation/bloom) |
| Second pour | 0:45 | 50% of total water — Switch CLOSED (immersion steep) |
| Drain | 2:00 | Open Switch; let drain |
| Done | 2:45–3:15 | Total brew time |

**Ratios:**
- 1:15 → entry-level conical grinder (Baratza Encore, Hario Skerton)
- 1:16–1:17 → consistent burr grinder

**"Sweet" variation:** Same 50/50 pour pattern, but close Switch at 0:25 instead of 0:45; open at 2:00. Reduces acidity, increases sweetness.

**Size scaling:**
| Size | Dose | Water | Close Switch | Open Switch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 20 g | 320 ml | 0:45 | 2:00 |
| Medium | 30 g | 480 ml | 1:00 | 2:30 |
| XL (03 Switch) | 45 g | 720 ml | 1:30 | 2:45 |

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- This article covers the **original Hario Switch** (glass V60 cone). The [[concepts/neo-switch]] page in this wiki refers to the newer "Neo Switch" model. The mechanism and recipes transfer directly, but the cone is different — see Neo Switch page for clarification.
- Christensen notes longer brew times when using natural Ethiopians — no specific adjusted recipe given. Implies grind coarsening or extended immersion is needed but leaves it open.
