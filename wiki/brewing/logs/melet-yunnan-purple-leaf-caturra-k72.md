# Melet — Yunnan 米良·觅月 (K72 "Purple-Leaf Caturra")

> First-party brew log (materialized from a Bean Conqueror export). The **brew tables are first-party measured data**; Ratio/EY are computed here at ingestion. All **bean-identity fields are as claimed** and carry **elevated commercial bias** — the Melet homepage could not be located, so the process/terroir detail below is quoted from *other* roasters' pages for the same coffee (flagged low-credibility). Do not assert any of it as entity fact (see Sourcing Discipline).

**⚠ Sourcing read — this is a textbook [[varietals/concepts/dr296|Dr296]] trap.** The label "紫叶卡杜拉 / Purple-Leaf Caturra," K72-washed, Yunnan **Xiding** (Menghai/Xishuangbanna), 1720–1800 m is *exactly* the fingerprint the wiki already flags as a **misnomer**: genetically this is a **Catimor** (Dr296), **not** a Bourbon-family [[varietals/concepts/caturra|Caturra]]. Every claimed attribute — variety name, K72 protocol, Xiding terroir, high altitude, "clean/tea-like" target — corroborates the [[varietals/concepts/dr296|Dr296]] profile. Treat cup expectations as **introgressed Catimor**, not delicate Caturra.

![[MELET Purple Leaf Caturra.jpg]]

## Bean (as claimed)

- **Variety:** 紫叶卡杜拉 "Purple-Leaf Caturra" → almost certainly **[[varietals/concepts/dr296|Dr296]]** ([[varietals/concepts/introgressed-varieties|Catimor]]).
- **Terroir:** Xiding Washing Station (西定), Menghai County, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan; 1720–1800 m. (Matches Dr296's documented growing regions.)
- **Process:** **K72 washed** — depulp with mucilage intact → 24 h dry fermentation (in bags) → rinse → secondary fermentation (dry day / wet night) to pH 4–4.1, **72 h total** → wash → **7-day** low-temp shade drying on raised beds. *(As quoted from ydselects.com for the same coffee — commercial, low-credibility; but it independently matches the K72 protocol already recorded on [[varietals/concepts/dr296|Dr296]].)*
- **Roast:** Melet, 2026-06-24; roast level 2/5; measured Agtron (whole) **63.5** — light-medium.
- **Roaster notes:** White Floral; Melon; Green Tea; Spice.

## Recommended Recipe (Melet, on label)

Ratio **1:13**, **89–92 °C**, **1–1.5 min**, target TDS **1.15–1.35%**. Brew water 50–110 ppm.

**Critique:** 1:13 is a very *tight* ratio — internally in tension with a 1.15–1.35% TDS target, which at 1:13 needs a **low EY (~15–18%)**, i.e. the fast 1–1.5 min contact is doing the work of keeping extraction down. In practice the Graycano pull *did* follow 1:13 and landed **TDS 1.82% / EY 19.7%** — over the TDS target. Subsequent pulls moved to **1:16–1:20 + bypass + dual-temp cold tails** to pull TDS into a drinkable band and chase clarity.

## Overall read

EY clusters **~20–23%**, TDS **1.37–1.82%** — consistently **strong and coffee-like**, matching CK's tasting note. [[varietals/concepts/dr296|Dr296]] is profiled as **clean/tea-like**; to get there, the levers are: **higher ratio** (toward 1:18–1:20) and/or **bypass** to bring TDS toward the roaster's 1.15–1.35%, plus **lower EY** — coarser grind, cooler main pour (~88 °C), shorter contact. The **dual-temp cold tail** is a keeper for finish/clarity; the single-temp Origami (EY 22.7%) is the counter-example to avoid.
