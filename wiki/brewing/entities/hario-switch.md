# Hario Switch

## What / Who

A coffee dripper by Hario that combines a V60 cone with a switch-valve base mechanism. The valve (steel ball + silicone base + plastic lever) allows toggling between immersion and pour-over (drip) modes during a single brew.

## Relevance

The Switch popularized hybrid brewing — sequential immersion and percolation — as a technique accessible to home brewers. It bridges the gap between the V60 (pour-over clarity) and the Clever Dripper (immersion body).

## Key Facts

- Uses 02-size V60 paper filters (Fact: source [[sources/hario-switch-review-coffee-chronicler]])
- Max capacity 250ml per fill (switch open allows continuous flow)
- 03-size variant exists but is rare; required for XL 45g recipe
- Cones: original = glass V60; newer model = Hario Neo cone (see [[concepts/neo-switch]])
- Switch mechanism feels slightly flimsy but reliable in daily use (Fact: [[sources/hario-switch-review-coffee-chronicler]])
