# Kaffeemacher

## What / Who

A Swiss (Basel) specialty-coffee company and education/media team, known for rigorous, data-driven coffee content (German-language YouTube + blog).

## Relevance

Ran the **24-espresso-grinder study** (video + blogpost) — retention, noise, price, blind tastings, and a deep particle-size-distribution dive — whose PSD data Jonathan Gagné analysed in [[brewing/sources/gagne-grinder-psd-analysis-2023]]. Their **blind tastings** (same coffee) are the taste axis in that analysis: top-rated grinders sat mid-range on unimodality/uniformity, implying PSD isn't the whole story.

## Key Facts
- Commissioned PSD measurement from [[brewing/entities/zhaw-coffee-excellence-center|ZHAW]] (Marco Wellinger) using one 82-point natural Brazilian coffee (source: [[brewing/sources/gagne-grinder-psd-analysis-2023]]).
- Covered 24 grinders across many dimensions, not just PSD (source: same).
