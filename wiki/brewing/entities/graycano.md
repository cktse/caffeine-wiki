# Graycano

## What / Who

German coffee gear manufacturer making the Graycano Dripper — a full metal cone dripper with a large exit hole, V-shaped ribs, and hollow chambers underneath for heat retention. Sustainability-focused brand. Has been featured in Brewers Cup competitions.

## Relevance

The Graycano's heat-sink design distinguishes it from other cone drippers. The hollow chambers capture hot air from the brewed coffee, maintaining stable brew temperature throughout the extraction.

## Key Facts

- Country of origin: Germany (Fact: [[sources/graycano-two-recipes-basic-barista]])
- Full metal construction; durable scratch-resistant coating
- Large exit hole → fast flow (cone mode)
- Hollow chamber base → heat retention (main engineering claim)
- **Graycano Coin** accessory: silicone insert that converts cone dripper to flat-bottom, enabling wave filters (Fact: [[sources/graycano-two-recipes-basic-barista]])
- Compatible filters: Graycano cone filter (own); Origami Wave Filter Small (with Coin)
- Available in Australia via The Basic Barista since 2022
