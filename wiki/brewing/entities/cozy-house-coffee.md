# Cozy House Coffee 暖窩咖啡

## What / Who

Taiwanese specialty-coffee education provider (founder 黃明志), running **SCA CSP** courses (Introduction, Barista, Brewing, Sensory, Roasting) across Taipei, Taichung, and Kaohsiung, plus a shop/blog.

## Relevance

Author of the well-reasoned teaching analysis of Bavis Kwong's **[[brewing/concepts/sandwich-extraction|sandwich extraction]]** — reframing a competition routine into transferable brewing principles (grind-distribution roles, sensory-first recipe design, roast↔brew coupling). Useful as a **practitioner/educator** voice; note **mild commercial bias** (sells courses, links equipment retailers) and that its recipe data is secondhand competition transcription (flagged as-claimed).

## Key Facts
- SCA-aligned education across three Taiwanese cities (source: [[brewing/sources/sandwich-extraction-bavis-kwong-cozyhouse]]).
- Analysis is transparent about data limits (215g vs 250g water; 94 vs 95 °C; unconfirmed re-roast rumor) — raises its credibility.
