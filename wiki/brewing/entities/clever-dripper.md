# Clever Dripper

## What / Who

Immersion coffee dripper with a steep-and-release valve mechanism. Uses trapezoid-shaped paper filters (widely available in supermarkets). Was the dominant "hybrid" brewer before the Hario Switch entered the market.

## Relevance

Referenced as the closest competitor to the Hario Switch. Key distinction: the Clever Dripper is primarily an immersion brewer; the Hario Switch enables true hybrid (pour-over + immersion) sequencing.

## Key Facts

- Uses trapezoid filters, not V60 cone filters (Fact: [[sources/hario-switch-review-coffee-chronicler]])
- Pure immersion steep-and-release; no built-in pour-over phase
- Produces a cleaner cup than French press due to paper filtration
