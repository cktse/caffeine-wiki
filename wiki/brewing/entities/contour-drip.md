# Contour Drip

## What / Who

A pour-over dripper by **Roastwork** (HK) with a **ceramic upper body** and a **silicone base**, six internal flow-guide ribs, designed for use with high-flow filter papers.

## Relevance

The dripper behind Bavis Kwong's **[[brewing/concepts/sandwich-extraction|sandwich extraction]]**. Its two-material body is used deliberately as a **thermal-gradient device**: the heat-retentive ceramic top drives the coarse layer's acidity, while the less-retentive silicone base spares the bottom fines from prolonged high heat — so different bed positions experience different temperatures even at one kettle setting.

## Key Facts
- Ceramic top + silicone base + six flow ribs; paired with two fast papers in the comp routine (source: [[brewing/sources/sandwich-extraction-bavis-kwong-cozyhouse]]).
- Not required for the technique: a standard V60/cone or a [[brewing/entities/hario-switch|Hario Switch]] can approximate the fines–paper–coarse layering; swapping drippers means re-testing temp/flow/time (source: same).
