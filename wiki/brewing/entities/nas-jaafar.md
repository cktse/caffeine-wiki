# Nas Jaafar

## What / Who

Malaysian coffee professional, **2026 World Brewers Cup champion** — the first Malaysian to win the title. Age 38; entered coffee in 2014 via front-of-house, became a Southeast Asia sales rep for green-coffee trader **Falcon Coffees**, competed from 2019, won the 2025 Malaysia Brewers Cup, then the 2026 world title.

## Relevance

His whole routine centres on one concept — **Resistance** (even water flow through the bed) — anchoring [[brewing/concepts/flow-resistance]]. A clean example of "brewing as the understanding game, not the talent game" (his path from waiter to world champion).

## Key Facts
- 2026 WBrC champion, 469 pts (source: [[brewing/sources/nas-jaafar-resistance-cozyhouse]], [[brewing/sources/clarity-pyramid-simon-gautherin-cozyhouse]]).
- Routine: 15g / 200g / 1:13 / 92 °C, UFO V3 on a Hario Switch base, WDT, ~700μm low-RPM grind; two-stage percolation→immersion, total 2:10 — as claimed (source: same).
- Bean: Panama Finca Nuguo Geisha, ~1,700 m, Natural Anaerobic — as claimed (source: same).
- Frames terroir as "resistance": slow high-altitude maturation + sealed anaerobic ferment → flavor density.
