# Comandante

## What / Who

German precision hand grinder manufacturer. Known for consistent grind size distribution across all grind sizes from espresso to cold brew. Two primary product lines: C40 and C60.

## Models

| Model | Burr size | Click resolution | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| C40 MK4 | Standard | ~30µm/click | Most widely used; Red Clix upgrade available |
| C40 + Red Clix (RX35) | Standard | ~15µm/click | Doubles adjustment granularity; replaces axle, spring, knob |
| C60 Baracuda | 60mm | 21µm/click | Full stainless steel body; ~1kg; GX50 Gold Clix drivetrain |

## Relevance

The Comandante C40 is the reference grinder for click counts used in Graycano recipes in this wiki (25 clicks cone, 30 clicks wave). The grind size chart provides the calibration between click counts and microns for all brew methods.

## Key Facts

- C40: each click ≈ 30µm; espresso range 3–6 clicks; pour-over range 20–30 clicks (Fact: [[sources/comandante-grind-size-chart]])
- Red Clix: multiplies click count × 2; primarily valuable for espresso dialing (Fact: [[sources/comandante-grind-size-chart]])
- C60: 21µm/click; C40 clicks × ~1.4 for equivalent C60 clicks (Fact: [[sources/comandante-grind-size-chart]])
