# Bavis Kwong 鄺嘉儀

## What / Who

Hong Kong coffee roaster and brewer at **Roastwork** (HK). 2025 Hong Kong Brewers Cup champion; **3rd place at the 2026 World Brewers Cup** (Brussels) on her first world-stage appearance.

## Relevance

Devised **[[brewing/concepts/sandwich-extraction|sandwich extraction]]** (particle-size stratification) — one of the most-discussed pour-over ideas of 2026: coarse on top for acidity/florals, a thin fines layer on the bottom (between two fast papers) for sweetness/body. Notable for treating fines as a **structural tool** rather than a defect, and for tightly co-designing roast and extraction.

## Key Facts
- 2025 HK Brewers Cup champion; 2026 WBrC 3rd (≈459 pts, inferred; top three within ~10 pts) (source: [[brewing/sources/sandwich-extraction-bavis-kwong-cozyhouse]]).
- Competition bean: Panama Janson Coffee Lot #251 **Green Tip Geisha**, natural; extreme short-development roast (FC ~8:30, DTR ~10%) — as claimed (source: same).
- Coached/credited: [[brewing/entities/tetsu-kasuya|Tetsu Kasuya]], Ching (Roastwork), Jhon Christopher (source: same).
- Routine theme: Hong Kong as East-meets-West — coarse and fine "coexisting, each well-treated."
