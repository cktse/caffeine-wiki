# Matt Perger

## What / Who

Australian barista and coffee educator; 2013 World Barista Championship finalist and World Brewers Cup champion, founder of Barista Hustle. Influential popularizer of several modern specialty techniques.

## Relevance

Credited with the global resurgence of the **sprover / café crème** (long, coarse, low-concentration "espresso-machine filter" drink) via the 2013 WBC — anchoring the sprover entry in [[brewing/concepts/espresso-shot-styles]].

## Key Facts
- Popularized the sprover at the 2013 WBC; later dubbed "Texas filter" in the US (source: [[brewing/sources/every-espresso-recipe-explained-hedrick]]).
- Typical sprover ratios ~1:8–1:12, very coarse grind, cooler temperature recommended (source: same).
