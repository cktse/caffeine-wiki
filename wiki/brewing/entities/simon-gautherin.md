# Simon Gautherin

## What / Who

Australia-based coffee professional; **2nd place at the 2026 World Brewers Cup** (Brussels), with the highest Open Service score of the final (346). Co-founder of the brewing-water brand **[[brewing/entities/apax-lab|APAX LAB]]** — a long-time water specialist, which shows in his precise mineral design.

## Relevance

Author of the **[[brewing/concepts/clarity-pyramid|Clarity Pyramid]]** framing and its **selective-extraction** technique (front-discard + tail cut-off) — a philosophy of *subtraction* (keep the legible flavors, remove what muddies them) rather than maximum extraction.

## Key Facts
- 2026 WBrC 2nd, 461 pts (Open Service 346 = highest of the final + Compulsory 115) (source: [[brewing/sources/clarity-pyramid-simon-gautherin-cozyhouse]]).
- Routine: 14g dual-Geisha (Colombia Peñas Blancas + Panama Finca Gales, 50/50), 200g, 89 °C, ~80ppm custom water, very coarse grind, NextLevel Pulsar; front ~10g discarded, tail cut early — as claimed (source: same).
- Water design: MgSO₄:KCl:SiO₂ ≈ 3:2:1 (citrus / stone-fruit / silky) — as claimed (source: same).
- Stated lesson: "trust your intuition"; his 10th year in Australia.
