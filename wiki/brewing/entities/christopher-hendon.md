# Christopher H. Hendon

## What / Who

Computational chemist (University of Oregon) known for peer-reviewed coffee science, including espresso extraction modelling and the book *Water for Coffee*. Publishes on the physical chemistry of brewing.

## Relevance

Co-author (with Michael Cameron and others) of the espresso work that catapulted the **turbo shot** — short (7–15 s), coarser-ground, higher-ratio shots whose EY rivals normal espresso at lower TDS, argued to extract more evenly. Anchors the turbo entry in [[brewing/concepts/espresso-shot-styles]].

## Key Facts
- Turbo shot parameters (as reported by [[brewing/sources/every-espresso-recipe-explained-hedrick]]): ~7–15 s, ~16 g dose, ~1:3 ratio; coarser grind → more even extraction (volcano-effect plotting).
- The paper's "flat 6-bar profile" is widely misread as a hardware requirement; in practice short fast shots can't build 6 bar regardless of OPV (source: same).
