# Hario V60 Drip Assist

## What / Who

An accessory that sits on top of the Hario V60 and distributes water evenly through small holes across the coffee bed. Acts as a water disperser, removing the need for a gooseneck kettle or precise spiral-pour technique.

## Relevance

Enables the 2026 "fast-filter" V60 recipe approach. By controlling water distribution mechanically rather than through technique, it makes higher-extraction V60 brewing repeatable for home brewers without competition-level pour control.

## Key Facts

- Sits on top of V60 during brewing; water is poured into/through it
- Disperses water through small holes → even saturation, low agitation
- Compatible with V60 01 and 02
- Works with any kettle — eliminates gooseneck kettle requirement (Fact: [[sources/basic-v60-recipe-2026]])
