# UFO V3

## What / Who

A pour-over dripper with a **shallow ~80° wall angle**, giving a wider, flatter coffee bed than a standard V60 cone. Used on a **Hario Switch** base for valve control in Nas Jaafar's 2026 WBrC routine.

## Relevance

The flat, wide bed keeps water from funnelling into a central column (as a steep cone can), supporting the champion's **even-resistance** goal — more uniform flow across the bed. Paired with the Switch valve, it enables percolation↔immersion sequencing.

## Key Facts
- ~80° wall angle → wider/shallower bed than V60; reduces centre-channelling tendency (source: [[brewing/sources/nas-jaafar-resistance-cozyhouse]]).
- Used on a Hario Switch base with WDT and ~700μm low-RPM grind (source: same).
