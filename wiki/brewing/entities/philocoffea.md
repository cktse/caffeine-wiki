# Philocoffea

## What / Who

Specialty coffee brand and shop founded by Tetsu Kasuya. Based in Funabashi City, Chiba Prefecture, Japan. Sells coffee equipment (including the Kasuya Model V60 dripper and Mini Drip Kettle Kasuya Model) and roasted beans.

## Relevance

The authoritative source for Kasuya's 4:6 Method documentation. Philocoffea's blog is where Kasuya publishes his own recipe descriptions and equipment recommendations.

## Key Facts

- Founded by [[entities/tetsu-kasuya]] — 2016 World Brewers Cup champion
- Produces Kasuya Model V60 — ribs removed at bottom to slow flow; designed for coarse grind with 4:6 method (Fact: [[sources/kasuya-46-method-philocoffea]])
- Produces Mini Drip Kettle Kasuya Model (300ml)
