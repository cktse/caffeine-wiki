# Scott Rao

## What / Who

Influential coffee author and consultant (the "Rao" of *The Professional Barista's Handbook*, *Everything but Espresso*, *The Coffee Roaster's Companion*). A dominant practitioner voice across roasting and brewing; widely cited in this wiki.

## Relevance

Anchors much of the wiki's espresso and roasting practice — the roast mass-loss → brew-ratio table, flow-rate × roast-level mechanism, transport/diffusion framing, light-roast strategies (Blooming/Allongé/Filter3), and astringency management. Credited by [[brewing/entities/lance-hendrick]] with popularizing the modern light-roast **allongé** in Montreal (~20 years ago).

## Key Facts
- Popularized high-flow, high-volume **allongé** for light roasts; ran ~150/day at his Montreal café (source: [[brewing/sources/rao-allonge-roast-flow-2025]]).
- Allongé figures: ~4.5 ml/s flow, **26–27% EY**, ratio 4:1–5:1 (source: same) — higher than Hendrick's stock-machine 22% at 1:5.6 (equipment/grind difference).
- Filter coffee TDS target **1.35–1.4%**; single-variable dialing (grind + end-dilution) discipline (source: [[brewing/sources/rao-blooming-filter3-quakers-2025]]).
- CGA as a large, less-readily-extracting molecule — reconciles with Gagné's filtration model of astringency (source: [[brewing/sources/rao-astringency-management-2020]]).
