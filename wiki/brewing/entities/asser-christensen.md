# Asser Christensen

## What / Who

Specialty coffee writer and YouTuber; founder of Coffee Chronicler (coffeechronicler.com). Known for detailed gear reviews and recipe development with a focus on light/Nordic-style roasts.

## Relevance

Developed the Coffee Chronicler Hario Switch recipe — the most widely circulated Switch recipe — which modified Tetsu Kasuya's "devil Switch" technique for higher extraction and lighter roasts.

## Key Facts

- Recipe design philosophy: consistency, flexibility, and acidity/fruit emphasis (Fact: [[sources/hario-switch-review-coffee-chronicler]])
- Publishes recipes and technique videos on YouTube alongside written reviews
