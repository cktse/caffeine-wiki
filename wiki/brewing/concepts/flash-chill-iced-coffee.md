# Flash-Chill Iced Coffee (急冷式)

## Definition

**Flash-chill** (Japanese 急冷式 *kyūrei*; "Japanese iced coffee") brews hot coffee **directly onto ice** so it cools rapidly. You extract fully at high temperature, then lock in aromatics by chilling instantly — the opposite of [[brewing/concepts/cold-brew|cold brew]] (long cold immersion). The recipe is a normal hot brew with the **water roughly halved** and the removed portion supplied as **ice** in the server.

## Why It Matters (in this domain)

- **Method matters even iced.** [[brewing/entities/tetsu-kasuya|Kasuya]] assumed that at matched strength iced coffees wouldn't differ much by brew method — then found a **clear** difference between a 4:6-style pour and a Switch-immersion hybrid (see recipes below). Brew structure, not just concentration, shapes the iced cup.
- **Two levers you already know.** Both recipes run on the **[[brewing/entities/hario-switch|Hario Switch]]**; you A/B them by changing only the pour method — pure percolation ([[brewing/concepts/46-method|4:6]]) vs. immersion hybrid.
- **A sweetness tool.** The hybrid's long closed steep pushes **rounded sweetness** — "sweet even though it's iced" — a useful counter to the thin/sharp profile flash-chill can otherwise give.

## The two Kasuya recipes

Shared base: **20 g coffee · 150 g hot water** (half the 4:6 standard 300 g) · **80 g ice** in the server · **28 clicks** grind → ~**1:7.5** hot ratio, then ice-melt dilutes to serving. Pour **onto the ice**.

### A — 4:6 applied (4:6応用の急冷)

Standard [[brewing/concepts/46-method|4:6]] with water halved: **30 g × 5** (was 60 g × 5), 90–93 °C, onto 80 g ice.
→ **Sharp acidity, defined outline.**

### B — Hybrid (ハイブリッド急冷)

Two pours using Switch immersion:

| Time | Switch | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open | 50 g, swirl to wet evenly |
| 0:40 | Closed | pour to 150 g, steep |
| 1:30 | Open | swirl, drain fully |

**Keep the temp up** (only two pours — cooling the water makes it thin).
→ **Smooth, cohesive, standout sweetness.**

## Choosing by bean (Kasuya)

| Goal / bean | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Draw out **clean acidity**; **Kenya / Ethiopia**, bright washed | **A — 4:6 flash-chill** |
| **Flashy** or **sweetness-forward** beans | **B — hybrid flash-chill** |

Neither is "correct" — pick for the bean's strength. Consistent with the wiki's origin/bean-led framing.

## Relationship to cold brew

Same goal (cold coffee), opposite mechanism. See [[brewing/concepts/cold-brew]] for the immersion route and the evidence base ([[brewing/sources/cold-brew-vs-iced-batali-2022|Batali et al. 2022]]). Flash-chill preserves the **hot-brew aromatic profile** (then chilled); cold brew produces a genuinely different, less-bitter/more-floral cup that is **not** sweeter in controlled study.
