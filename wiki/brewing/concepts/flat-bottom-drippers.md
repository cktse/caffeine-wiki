# Flat-Bottom Drippers

## Definition

A family of pour-over drippers with a **flat bed** (vs the cone of a V60), so the coffee sits shallower and wider and water passes more **uniformly** across it. Collated here as one page to avoid a page per make — the individual products are variations on a few axes, not distinct concepts.

**Defining trait — reduced/zero bypass.** Cone drippers (V60) let some water slip down the wall past the bed; most flat-bottoms are designed to force water **through** the bed (**no-bypass**), which makes ratio↔extraction more repeatable and tends to push **extraction yield up**. Contrast [[concepts/v60|V60]] (cone, bypass, pour-technique-sensitive).

## Where it sits in the model

- **Y (clarity ↔ body): → clarity / evenness / forgiving.** A flatter, more uniform bed reduces channeling and the pour-skill dependence of a cone → cleaner, more repeatable extraction. Widely reported as "balanced, forgiving" ([[sources/harmony-pourover-recipes-2026]]).
- **X (balance): nudges EY up.** No-bypass sends all the water through the grounds, so for a given grind you extract more — useful for less-soluble **light roasts**, a risk (over-extraction) as roast darkens. See the bias note below.
- Pairs naturally with [[concepts/fast-filters|fast filters]] (e.g. Orea + Sibarist) to allow a finer grind without stalling, and with the [[concepts/bed-depth|bed-depth]] logic (diameter sets batch at a target depth — the [[entities/nextlevel-pulsar|Pulsar]]/Pulsar Mini pair is exactly this).

## Variation axes (not separate concepts)

| Axis | Options | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Size / diameter** | 155 / 185 / 03 etc. | batch size at a target [[concepts/bed-depth\|bed depth]] — a *size* choice, not flavour |
| **Shower screen / dispersion cap** | present ([[entities/nextlevel-pulsar\|Pulsar]]) vs none (Orea, Kalita) | softens pour turbulence → more even wetting |
| **Valve** | present ([[entities/nextlevel-pulsar\|Pulsar]], Switch-style) vs none | adds immersion / steep-then-release ([[concepts/neo-switch\|hybrid]]) |
| **Filter geometry** | wavy (Kalita) vs flat (Orea/FLO) | wave paper reduces wall contact; flat needs a fast paper |

## Bias / grounding note

Flat-bottoms are **under-covered in this wiki so far** — a deliberate, disclosed bias, **not** a judgement:
- **I don't own one** — every other brewing page is backed by hands-on logs; there are no flat-bottom brew logs to draw on.
- The one **grounded** flat-bottom source here is Gagné's [[concepts/bed-depth|bed-depth]] work (Pulsar/Pulsar Mini). The rest of the "flat-bottom = higher extraction / more balanced" reputation is **forum hearsay**, not evidence.
- The **higher-EY** expectation is *mechanically plausible* (no-bypass → all water through the bed), and would suggest flat-bottoms suit **lighter roasts** best while risking over-extraction on medium/dark — but this is **unverified** and pending a first-hand experiment (would need to buy one). Treat as **provisional**.
