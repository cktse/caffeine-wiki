# Fast-Flowing Filters

## Definition

A category of pour-over filter papers engineered for higher flow rate than traditional papers. Reduced resistance through the filter bed allows finer grinds to be used without clogging, enabling higher extraction at shorter brew times.

## Why It Matters

Traditional V60 filter papers impose a flow-rate ceiling that forces medium-coarse grind to avoid stalling. Fast filters remove this constraint: grind finer → more surface area → higher extraction efficiency → more clarity and sweetness without bitterness or astringency.

This changes the recipe design space. With a fast filter, the conventional "V60 = medium-coarse grind" assumption no longer holds.

## How Fast Filters Change the System

Without fast filter (traditional):
- Grind: medium-coarse to coarse
- Temp: 88–93°C by roast
- Brew time: 3:00–3:30
- Technique: gooseneck kettle + precise pour required

With fast filter (2026 approach):
- Grind: fine (closer to table salt; ~15–20 C40 clicks estimated)
- Temp: 98°C
- Brew time: 2:00–2:30
- Technique: Drip Assist removes gooseneck dependency

The fast filter is the enabling variable. Without it, 98°C + fine grind would clog the bed and over-extract.
