# Graycano

## What It Is

German-made full metal cone dripper with a large exit hole for fast flow and V-shaped ribs. Its primary engineering innovation is a hollow chamber base that captures hot air from the brewed coffee, acting as a heat sink to maintain stable brew temperature. Has appeared in Brewers Cup competitions.

> **Correction from bootstrap:** The Graycano is a cone dripper with fast flow — not flat-bottom, not restricted outflow, not semi-immersion. Flat-bottom brewing requires the optional Graycano Coin adaptor (see below).

## Why It Matters

Temperature stability is the Graycano's claim. Most drippers lose heat to the ambient air and to the dripper body itself; the Graycano's hollow-chamber base recaptures and holds heat. This means less temperature drop mid-brew without needing to pre-heat aggressively.

## Two Brewing Modes

| Mode | Filter | Flow | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cone** (default) | Graycano cone filter | Fast (large exit hole) | Higher extraction; body; texture |
| **Wave / Flat-bottom** (with Coin) | Origami Wave Filter Small | Slower (Coin restricts) | Even, clean; coarser grind |

**Graycano Coin** — a silicone adaptor inserted inside the dripper that creates a flat base to support wave/flat-bottom filters. Slows flow. Use a fast-flowing filter (e.g. Origami Wave) to avoid too-long brew times.

## Cone Recipe (The Basic Barista)

- **Dose:** 15 g
- **Water:** 250 g (1:16.7)
- **Temperature:** 95°C
- **Grind:** ~25 clicks Comandante C40
- **Structure:** 5 equal pours of 50 g; wait for near-full drawdown between each
- No explicit timing — pour-driven not clock-driven
- **Best for:** Washed coffees; adds body and texture at higher extraction

## Wave/Flat-bottom Recipe (with Graycano Coin)

- **Dose:** 15 g
- **Water:** 250 g (1:16.7)
- **Temperature:** 98°C
- **Grind:** ~30 clicks Comandante C40 (coarser than cone mode)
- **Structure:** 50 g bloom → 100 g → 100 g (3 pours total; wait for drawdown between)
- **Best for:** Any coffee; cleaner, more even cup; suits coffees where clarity is the goal

## Mode vs. Grind vs. Temperature

- Cone mode uses finer grind (25 clicks) at lower temp (95°C) → higher extraction, more body
- Wave mode uses coarser grind (30 clicks) at higher temp (98°C) → slower flow compensated by coarser grind; cleaner result
- The temperature difference between modes (95 vs. 98°C) is notable — the Coin's slower flow likely enables the higher temp without over-extracting
