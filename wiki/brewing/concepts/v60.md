# Hario V60 (Ceramic)

## What It Is

Conical dripper with a single large hole and spiral ribs. Flow rate is controlled entirely by the brewer's pour speed and grind size. Ceramic version retains heat better than plastic.

## Why It Matters

The V60 is unforgiving but transparent — it reveals the coffee clearly. Ideal for highlighting acidity and floral aromatics in washed light roasts.

## Kasuya 4:6 Method (primary recipe)

The recipe that won the 2016 World Brewers Cup. See [[concepts/46-method]] for full detail. Summary:

- **Dose/ratio:** 20g / 300g (1:15); each pour = dose × 3g
- **Grind:** Medium-coarse to coarse
- **Temp:** ~93°C light / ~88°C medium / ~83°C dark (Fact: [[sources/kasuya-46-method-philocoffea]])
- **Structure:** 5 pours of 60g each at ~45-sec intervals; wait for near-full drain before each pour
- **Flavor control:** adjust ratio of first two pours (40% phase) — first < second = sweeter; first > second = brighter
- **Strength control:** number of pours in last three (60% phase) — fewer pours = lighter body

## 2026 Fast-Filter Recipe (Basic Barista)

A different system enabled by fast-flowing filters (Hario Meteor or Sibarist) and the Hario V60 Drip Assist accessory. Inverts the conventional grind wisdom — uses fine grind at high temp for a faster, higher-extraction brew.

- **Dose/ratio:** 20g / 300g (1:15)
- **Grind:** Fine — "closer to table salt", finer than traditional V60 (~15–20 C40 clicks estimated)
- **Temp:** 98°C (fixed, regardless of roast)
- **Filter:** Fast-flowing filter required (Hario Meteor or Sibarist) — standard filters will clog at this grind
- **Accessory:** Hario V60 Drip Assist (eliminates need for gooseneck kettle)
- **Total time:** 2:00–2:30

| Time | Action | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Bloom 50g | 50g |
| 0:40 | Pour to 100g | 100g |
| 1:00 | Pour to 150g | 150g |
| 1:20 | Pour to 225g | 225g |
| 1:40 | Pour to 300g | 300g |

**Cup character:** Higher extraction, cleaner finish, pronounced acidity, clear flavor separation. See [[sources/basic-v60-recipe-2026]].

## Two-Recipe Comparison

| | Kasuya 4:6 | Basic Barista 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Grind | Medium-coarse to coarse | Fine (table salt) |
| Temp | 93/88/83°C by roast | 98°C fixed |
| Total time | ~3:30 | 2:00–2:30 |
| Filter | Standard V60 | Fast filter required |
| Equipment | Standard V60 + gooseneck | V60 + Drip Assist + fast filter |
| Cup target | Adjustable (sweet/bright/strong) | High extraction, clarity, acidity |

These are not contradictory — they are different systems with different equipment and flavor targets.

## Technique Notes (traditional)
- Bloom with 2× dose weight (30–45 sec), then pour in slow controlled circles
- Multiple pours vs. continuous pour shifts body vs. clarity
- Avoid pouring on the filter wall
- 4:6: no explicit bloom — first pour serves as combined bloom and extraction

## Best For
- Washed Ethiopia / Kenya / Colombia
- Coffees with high intrinsic acidity and clarity
