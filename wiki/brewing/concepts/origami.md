# Origami Dripper

## What It Is

Japanese-designed dripper with 20 ridged folds (origami pattern) allowing the use of either cone filters (V60-style) or flat-bottom (Kalita-style) filters. Ceramic or resin versions available.

## Why It Matters

The ridge design creates an air gap between filter and dripper walls, promoting even flow regardless of filter shape. Versatile: technique and filter choice shift the brew toward clarity (cone) or body (flat-bottom).

## Base Recipe (starting point — cone filter)
- **Dose**: 15 g coffee
- **Water**: 240 ml (1:16)
- **Water temp**: 90–94°C
- **Grind**: Medium-fine
- **Total time**: 2:30–3:15

## Technique Notes
- With cone filter: behaves close to V60; good clarity
- With flat-bottom filter: closer to Kalita; fuller body, more even extraction
- Origami stand matters — use the correct size stand for stable flow

## Best For
- Flexible — works well with a wide range of coffees and roast levels
